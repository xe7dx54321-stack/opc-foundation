Item 1. Business

Apple Inc. ("Apple" or "the Company") designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables and accessories, and sells a variety of related services.

Products

The Company's products include iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch and AirPods.

Services

The Company's services include Advertising, AppleCare, Cloud Services, Digital Content and Payment Services.
